Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026. This page describes what this website actually does. It is a plain-language summary, not legal advice.

Who we are

This website is operated by Qywam, an AI-native advertising and creative company. To ask about your data, use the contact form.

Information you send us

If you use the contact form we receive the name, email address and message you type. We use them only to reply to your enquiry. The message is sent by email through our email delivery provider (Resend) and is not published.

Analytics and cookies

The website does not set advertising cookies. If Google Analytics 4 is enabled, it loads only after you click “Accept analytics” in the consent banner, and measures page views and form submissions. If you decline, no analytics script is loaded. Your choice is stored in your browser’s local storage under the key qywam-consent.

Third-party services

  • Vercel hosts the website and may process technical request data such as IP address and user agent in server logs.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces used on the site, which means your browser requests them from Google.
  • Resend delivers contact form messages to us.
  • Google Analytics, only if you accept analytics.

Spam protection

The contact form uses a hidden field, a minimum completion time and per-IP rate limiting to reduce abuse. IP addresses are used for rate limiting and are not stored with your message.

Your choices

You can decline or clear analytics consent by clearing this site’s local storage in your browser. To request access to or deletion of a message you sent us, contact us through the contact form.

Changes

We will update this page and its date if our practices change.
